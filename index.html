Our Treatments

Dental Implants

Process: Consultation → Surgical Placement → Osseointegration (3-6 mo) → Final Crown.

Recovery: 1-3 days of mild swelling. Most return to work the next day.

Orthodontics & Aligners

Process: 3D Scan → Custom ClinCheck Plan → Bi-weekly aligner changes or monthly brace adjustments.

Duration: 6 to 18 months on average, depending on complexity.

Pediatric Dentistry

Process: Gentle, tell-show-do exam, fluoride varnish application, and protective sealants.

Environment: Kid-friendly, gamified experience with a dedicated play area.
